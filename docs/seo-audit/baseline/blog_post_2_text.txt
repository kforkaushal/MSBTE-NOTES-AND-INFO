🕯️ Remembering the Legend
Born as
Vishwanath Patekar
on January 1, 1951, in Murud-Janjira, Maharashtra, Nana
                Patekar rose from humble beginnings to become one of the most respected and feared actors in Indian
                cinema. His journey from a small-town boy to a National Award-winning superstar is nothing short of
                extraordinary.
🎭 Life at a Glance
📅

With his passing, India has lost not just an actor but a cultural force — a man whose raw
                intensity on screen was matched only by his compassion off it.
🎬 A Career That Defined Intensity
Spanning over
four decades
and more than
100 films
, Nana Patekar's
                career was a masterclass in raw, unfiltered acting. He never relied on glamour — his weapon was pure,
                undiluted talent.
🔥 The Angry Man of Indian Cinema
His portrayal of Pratap Lakshman Narayanrao Panpatil in
"Krantiveer"
(1994) became a cultural phenomenon. The courtroom monologue is still considered one of the most
                        powerful scenes in Bollywood history, earning him the National Award for Best Actor.
🎭 The Versatile Artist
From the menacing villain in
"Agni Sakshi"
to the lovable comic
                        timing in
"Welcome"
, Nana Patekar proved that true talent knows no genre boundaries. He
                        could make audiences laugh and cry in the same film.
🌟 Marathi Cinema's Crown Jewel
His magnum opus
"Natsamrat"
(2016) is widely regarded as one of the
                        finest Marathi films ever made. His portrayal of the aging Shakespearean actor
Ganpat
                            Ramchandra Belwalkar
left audiences in tears and set new benchmarks for regional cinema.

🎥 Iconic Movies & Unforgettable Dialogues
Nana Patekar's dialogues weren't just lines — they were thunderbolts. Here are some of the films and moments
                that defined his legacy:
Krantiveer (1994)
"Ek machhar, aadmi ko hijda bana deta hai..."
The iconic courtroom monologue that became a rallying cry against social injustice.
Welcome (2007)
"Aapka ghoda kitne ka hai?"
As Uday Shetty, he delivered comedy gold that still makes people burst into laughter.
Natsamrat (2016)
"Mee Natsamrat! Ganpat Ramchandra Belwalkar!"
A devastating performance that redefined Marathi cinema and brought audiences to tears.
Ab Tak Chhappan (2004)
As encounter specialist Sadhu Agashe, he portrayed the morally grey hero with chilling authenticity.
The Attacks of 26/11 (2013)
His portrayal of the brave police officer during the Mumbai terror attacks was both harrowing and heroic.
🤝 Beyond Cinema — The Real Hero
While the world saw Nana Patekar as a fearsome actor on screen, his real-life persona was one of deep
                compassion and humility. His work off-screen may be even more impactful than his films.
🌾 Naam Foundation
Nana Patekar founded the
Naam Foundation
to help drought-affected
                    farmers in Maharashtra. The foundation has provided direct financial assistance, built water storage
                    systems, and brought hope to thousands of farming families across rural Maharashtra.
Provided homes to
thousands of flood-affected families
Built water conservation structures across drought-hit villages
Distributed farming equipment and seeds to struggling farmers
Personally visited disaster-hit areas to coordinate relief
"I don't act in real life. The real Nana Patekar is the one
                    who works for farmers, not the one who shouts on screen."
— Nana Patekar
🧑‍🌾 A Man of the People
Despite being one of Bollywood's biggest stars, Nana Patekar was known for
                    his simplicity. He lived in a modest home, wore simple clothes, and donated most of his earnings to
                    charity. His humility and dedication to social causes made him a true people's hero.
🇮🇳 A Nation Mourns
The news of Nana Patekar's passing has sent shockwaves across the nation. Tributes have poured in from
                every corner — from political leaders to fellow actors, from filmmakers to the common people who saw him
                as their own.
🎬 Film Industry
Colleagues across Bollywood and Marathi cinema have expressed their grief,
                        remembering his generosity, intensity, and the mentorship he provided to younger actors. Many have
                        called him "irreplaceable" and "the last of the real actors."
🌾 Farming Community
Thousands of farmers who were directly helped by Nana Patekar's Naam
                        Foundation have gathered to pay their respects. For them, he wasn't a celebrity — he was family.
                        His work for the farming community will continue to inspire generations.
🙏 Public Tributes
Social media has been flooded with emotional tributes and clips of his most
                        famous scenes. The hashtag #NanaPatekar has been trending with millions sharing their favourite
                        memories and dialogues.
🕯️
Your Art Will Live On Forever 🎭❤️
His powerful performances, unforgettable dialogues, and unique acting style touched millions of hearts.
                    His contribution to Marathi and Hindi cinema will always be remembered.
Heartfelt Condolences. May His Soul Rest in Peace. 🕊️🙏
