Evaluation Blueprint

How MSBTE Answers Are Evaluated by Examiners

Understanding the official scoring keys to maximize theory examination marks.

1

Step-Wise Partial Credit

MSBTE examiners allocate specific marks for each distinct phase of a solution: 1 mark for formula definition, 2 marks for substitution, and 1 mark for final answer with units.

2

Diagrams & Pinouts Weightage

Every engineering question with architecture, flowcharts, or timing diagrams carries up to 50% of the total question marks purely for clear, labeled illustrations.

3

Tabular Comparisons

When asked to "Differentiate", writing comparison points in neat tables with parameters (e.g., speed, complexity, protocol) guarantees full 4/4 or 6/6 marks according to model keys.
