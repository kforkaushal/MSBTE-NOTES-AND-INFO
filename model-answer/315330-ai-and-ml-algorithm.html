MSBTE Step-Wise Marking Methodology for AML

MSBTE examiners evaluate answer sheets based on rigorous step-wise marking guidelines issued with each model answer paper. Here is how marks are distributed across question types:

Diagrams & Schematics

Up to 40%–50% of total marks in a question are reserved for a correct, labelled diagram. Always draw diagrams in dark pencil with distinct pin/node labels.

Formulas & Derivations

Stating the governing equation earns 1 mark, substitution earns 1–2 marks, and correct arithmetic with SI units receives final marks.

Code & Algorithm Syntax

Minor syntax errors are forgiven if algorithm logic, comments, and data flow are intact. Always include input, process, and output stages.
