Mastering First Year Diploma (Sem 1 & 2) Under MSBTE K-Scheme

The First Year of Diploma Engineering lays the foundation for all specialized branches including Computer Technology, IT, Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, and Electronics. Under the updated MSBTE K-Scheme curriculum, the evaluation balances theoretical knowledge with hands-on practical competencies and continuous assessments.

1. Formula Mastery

Subjects like Basic Science (311305) and Applied Mathematics (312301) require memorization of core formulas and daily problem practice.

2. Practical Journals

Lab manuals and workshop practices carry high internal weightage. Submit verified records on time to earn full continuous assessment marks.

3. Unit Test Strategy

Unit tests directly contribute to your internal assessment average. Revise unit-wise notes 3 days prior to guarantee high sessional scores.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which subjects are in MSBTE Semester 1? ↓

Semester 1 includes Basic Science (311305), Communication Skills in English (311303), and branch-specific Engineering Workshop Practice (311002, 311005, 311007, 311010).

Which subjects are in MSBTE Semester 2? ↓

Semester 2 covers Applied Mathematics (312301), Programming in C (312303), Basic Electrical & Electronics (312302), Engineering Mechanics (312312), Web Page Designing (312004), Linux Basics (312001), and branch foundations.

How can I download study materials from this page? ↓

Click on the "Explore Notes & PDF" button on any subject card to view live interactive PDF previews and download files directly via Google Drive.
