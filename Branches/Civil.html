About MSBTE Diploma in Civil Engineering (CE)

The Civil Engineering polytechnic diploma program under MSBTE K-Scheme prepares students in structural design, surveying, geotechnical engineering, building construction materials, concrete technology, and highway infrastructure planning. It emphasizes field surveying methods and computerized CAD drafting.

Core Civil Engineering Subjects

  • First Year: Basic Science, Mathematics, Engineering Graphics, Civil Workshop, and Applied Mechanics.
  • Second Year: Surveying, Building Material & Construction, Concrete Technology, Mechanics of Structures (MOS), Hydraulics, and Geotechnical Engineering.
  • Third Year: Design of Steel & RCC Structures, Estimating Costing & Valuation, Highway & Railway Engineering, Contracts & Accounts, and Environmental Engineering.

Exam Preparation & Lab Manuals

Civil engineering examinations require precision in structural formulas, bending moment calculations, and neat schematic cross-sections. Access our verified question banks, model answers, CAD drawings, and formula sheets for every K-Scheme semester.
