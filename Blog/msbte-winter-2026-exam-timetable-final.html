MSBTE Winter 2026 FINAL Theory Exam Timetable Declared | All Diploma Branches

Published by MSBTE Notes & Info Team · November 14, 2026

Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE), Mumbai has officially published the FINAL Time Table for the Winter 2026 Theory Examination. Explore day-wise dates (Nov 17 - Dec 09), morning/afternoon slots, K-Scheme & I-Scheme codes, and institute download guide.

1. Official Board Release & Exam Overview

The Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education, Mumbai (Headquartered at Government Polytechnic Building, 49, Kherwadi, Bandra East, Mumbai 400 051) has officially published the FINAL Time Table for Winter 2026 Theory Examinations. This comprehensive timetable applies to all students pursuing Diploma in Engineering & Technology, Pharmacy, and Government Approved Short-Term (Non-AICTE) Courses across the state.

The theory examinations will run continuously for 18 working exam days, beginning on Tuesday, November 17, 2026 and ending on Wednesday, December 09, 2026.

17 Nov 2026 Exam Start Date
09 Dec 2026 Exam End Date
18 Days Total Duration
2 Shifts Morning & Afternoon

2. Crucial Board Guidelines for Winter 2026 Candidates

  • • 18-Day Schedule: The Exam Day & Date wise FINAL Time Table is displayed on the MSBTE portal for students, affiliated polytechnic institutes, and examination centers.
  • • Old Schemes Equivalence: All students belonging to legacy schemes (I-Scheme, G-Scheme, E-Scheme, C-Scheme) must appear for the theory examination as per the paper codes offered as equivalent to their courses, which are printed explicitly on their hall ticket.
  • • Clash Policy: Examinations of different semesters or years scheduled on the same calendar day but in different sessions (Morning vs. Afternoon) are NOT considered a clash.
  • • Special 4-Hour Papers: Major design, drawing, and structural papers (such as Theory of Structure, Estimating & Costing, Engineering Drawing, Machine Design, and Production Drawing) are allocated 4 hours (Morning: 09:30 AM to 01:30 PM; Afternoon: 02:00 PM to 06:00 PM).

4. How to Download Your Institute-Wise Timetable

Students can also verify their college-specific examination schedule via the MSBTE online portal:

  1. Visit the official MSBTE timetable link: https://online.msbte.co.in/timetable/institutewise
  2. Select Institute-Wise, Course-Wise, or Day-Wise view.
  3. Enter your 4-digit Institute Code (e.g., 0061 for Smt. Sharadchandrika Suresh Patil Institute of Technology, Chopda).
  4. Click Submit to download or print your customized center date sheet.
