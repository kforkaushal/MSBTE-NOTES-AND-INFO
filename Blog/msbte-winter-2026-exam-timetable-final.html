MSBTE Winter 2026 FINAL Theory Exam Timetable Declared | All Diploma Branches

By MSBTE Notes & Info Team • November 14, 2026 • 8 min read

The Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE), Mumbai has officially published the FINAL Time Table for the Winter 2026 Theory Examination. The 18-day examination kicks off on 17th November 2026 and runs through 09th December 2026 for all Diploma in Engineering, Technology, Pharmacy, and Non-AICTE government approved courses across Maharashtra.

🏛️ Board Headquarters Official Release

Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education, Mumbai

Government Polytechnic Building, 49, Kherwadi, Bandra (E), Mumbai 400 051.
Examination: Winter 2026 Theory Examination (FINAL) for Diploma in Engineering, Pharmacy, and Government Approved Short Term (Non-AICTE) Courses.

K-Scheme Full Coverage I-Scheme & G-Scheme Equivalent Status: 100% FINAL
17 Nov Exam Start Date
09 Dec Exam End Date
18 Days Total Duration
2 Slots Morning & Afternoon

📌 Important Board Instructions for Winter 2026 Candidates

  • • 18-Day Working Exam Window: The Winter 2026 Theory Examination spans exactly 18 exam days without unexpected scheduling changes.
  • • Old Schemes Equivalence: All students belonging to legacy schemes (I-Scheme, G-Scheme, E-Scheme, and C-Scheme) must appear as per the paper codes offered as equivalent to their courses, which are clearly printed on their official hall ticket.
  • • Same-Day Clash Policy: Examinations of different semesters/years scheduled on the same calendar day but in different sessions (Morning vs. Afternoon) are NOT considered a clash.
  • • Special 4-Hour Papers: Notice that design, drawing, and structural papers (such as Theory of Structure, Design of Machine Elements, Estimating & Costing, Engineering Drawing, and Production Drawing) are allotted 4 full hours (Morning: 09:30 AM to 01:30 PM; Afternoon: 02:00 PM to 06:00 PM).

Branch-Wise Schedule Breakdown & Exam Strategy

💻 Computer Engineering & Information Technology (CO & IF)

Computer & IT diploma students face a critical schedule starting on Day 1 with Operating Systems (315319 / 22516). Key core subjects appear early:

  • Day 1 (17-Nov): Operating Systems (315319 / 22516) in the Afternoon Session.
  • Day 2 (18-Nov): Data Structures Using C (313301) for 3rd semester candidates.
  • Day 3 (19-Nov): Java Programming (314317 / 22412) in the Morning Session.
  • Day 5 (21-Nov): Database Management System (313302) & Digital Forensics / Machine Learning electives.
  • Day 8 (26-Nov): Digital Techniques (313303) and Python Programming (22616).
  • Day 13 (03-Dec): Programming in C (312303) for 2nd semester backlogs.

🏗️ Civil Engineering (CE)

Civil engineering students must pay close attention to 4-hour papers involving structural calculations and drawings:

  • Day 1 (17-Nov): Theory of Structure (315313) - 4-hour afternoon paper (02:00 PM to 06:00 PM).
  • Day 2 (18-Nov): Strength of Materials (313308) in the Afternoon session.
  • Day 3 (19-Nov): Estimating, Costing & Valuation (314313) - 4-hour morning paper (09:30 AM to 01:30 PM).
  • Day 5 (21-Nov): Design of RCC and Steel Structures (316308) - 4-hour morning slot.
  • Day 15 (05-Dec): Building Planning and Drawing (22405) - 4-hour morning session.

⚡ Electrical Engineering (EE)

High-weightage machine, circuits, and power system papers are evenly spaced across the schedule:

  • Day 1 (17-Nov): A.C. Machines Performance (315333 / 22523) in Afternoon session.
  • Day 2 (18-Nov): Electrical Circuits and Network (313332) in Afternoon session.
  • Day 4 (20-Nov): Switchgear and Protection (315334 / 22524) in Afternoon session.
  • Day 6 (23-Nov): D.C. Machines and Transformers (314322) in Morning slot.

⚙️ Mechanical Engineering (ME)

Mechanical diploma candidates have design and drawing examinations with extended durations:

  • Day 2 (18-Nov): Design of Machine Elements (316357) - 4-hour morning paper.
  • Day 5 (21-Nov): Production Drawing (313311) - 4-hour afternoon slot (02:00 PM to 06:00 PM).
  • Day 7 (25-Nov): Engineering Drawing (312311) - 4-hour morning paper.
  • Day 14 (04-Dec): Fluid Mechanics and Machinery (313309) in the Afternoon session.

Frequently Asked Questions (MSBTE Winter 2026 Timetable)

When will the MSBTE Winter 2026 theory exams start and end? ▼

The MSBTE Winter 2026 theory examinations officially start on November 17, 2026 (Day 1) and end on December 09, 2026 (Day 18), conducting examinations over an 18-day timeframe across Maharashtra.

Is this MSBTE Winter 2026 timetable tentative or final? ▼

This is the FINAL Time Table issued by the Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education, Mumbai. The schedule is confirmed for all AICTE and Non-AICTE diploma courses.

What are the exact morning and afternoon shift timings? ▼

Standard papers run for 3 hours: Morning session from 09:30 AM to 12:30 PM and Afternoon session from 02:00 PM to 05:00 PM. Special drawing and structural design papers are allocated 4 hours (Morning: 09:30 AM to 01:30 PM; Afternoon: 02:00 PM to 06:00 PM).

What should old scheme students (I-Scheme, G-Scheme) do? ▼

Old scheme students will appear for equivalent paper codes offered as replacements for their courses. Your exact paper codes and scheduled exam slots are printed explicitly on your MSBTE Hall Ticket.

How can I check my institute-wise or college-specific schedule? ▼

You can visit the official portal at online.msbte.co.in/timetable/institutewise, enter your 4-digit Institute Code, and get your personalized college examination center timetable.
