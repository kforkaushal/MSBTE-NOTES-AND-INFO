🕯️ Remembering the Legend

Born as Vishwanath Patekar on January 1, 1951, in Murud-Janjira, Maharashtra, Nana Patekar rose from humble beginnings to become one of the most respected and feared actors in Indian cinema. His journey from a small-town boy to a National Award-winning superstar is nothing short of extraordinary.

🎭 Life at a Glance

  • 📅 Born: January 1, 1951 — Murud-Janjira, Maharashtra
  • 🎬 Full Name: Vishwanath Patekar
  • 🏆 National Awards: 3 (Best Actor & Best Supporting Actor)
  • 🎂 Age: 75 years
  • 🎥 Career Span: Over 4 decades (1978–2026)

With his passing, India has lost not just an actor but a cultural force — a man whose raw intensity on screen was matched only by his compassion off it.

🎬 A Career That Defined Intensity

Spanning over four decades and more than 100 films, Nana Patekar's career was a masterclass in raw, unfiltered acting. He never relied on glamour — his weapon was pure, undiluted talent.

🔥 The Angry Man of Indian Cinema

His portrayal of Pratap Lakshman Narayanrao Panpatil in "Krantiveer" (1994) became a cultural phenomenon. The courtroom monologue is still considered one of the most powerful scenes in Bollywood history, earning him the National Award for Best Actor.

🎭 The Versatile Artist

From the menacing villain in "Agni Sakshi" to the lovable comic timing in "Welcome", Nana Patekar proved that true talent knows no genre boundaries. He could make audiences laugh and cry in the same film.

🌟 Marathi Cinema's Crown Jewel

His magnum opus "Natsamrat" (2016) is widely regarded as one of the finest Marathi films ever made. His portrayal of the aging Shakespearean actor Ganpat Ramchandra Belwalkar left audiences in tears and set new benchmarks for regional cinema.

🎥 Iconic Movies & Unforgettable Dialogues

Nana Patekar's dialogues weren't just lines — they were thunderbolts. Here are some of the films and moments that defined his legacy:

Krantiveer (1994)

"Ek machhar, aadmi ko hijda bana deta hai..."

The iconic courtroom monologue that became a rallying cry against social injustice.

Welcome (2007)

"Aapka ghoda kitne ka hai?"

As Uday Shetty, he delivered comedy gold that still makes people burst into laughter.

Natsamrat (2016)

"Mee Natsamrat! Ganpat Ramchandra Belwalkar!"

A devastating performance that redefined Marathi cinema and brought audiences to tears.

Ab Tak Chhappan (2004)

As encounter specialist Sadhu Agashe, he portrayed the morally grey hero with chilling authenticity.

The Attacks of 26/11 (2013)

His portrayal of the brave police officer during the Mumbai terror attacks was both harrowing and heroic.

🤝 Beyond Cinema — The Real Hero

While the world saw Nana Patekar as a fearsome actor on screen, his real-life persona was one of deep compassion and humility. His work off-screen may be even more impactful than his films.

🌾 Naam Foundation

Nana Patekar founded the Naam Foundation to help drought-affected farmers in Maharashtra. The foundation has provided direct financial assistance, built water storage systems, and brought hope to thousands of farming families across rural Maharashtra.

  • Provided homes to thousands of flood-affected families
  • Built water conservation structures across drought-hit villages
  • Distributed farming equipment and seeds to struggling farmers
  • Personally visited disaster-hit areas to coordinate relief

"I don't act in real life. The real Nana Patekar is the one who works for farmers, not the one who shouts on screen."

— Nana Patekar

🧑‍🌾 A Man of the People

Despite being one of Bollywood's biggest stars, Nana Patekar was known for his simplicity. He lived in a modest home, wore simple clothes, and donated most of his earnings to charity. His humility and dedication to social causes made him a true people's hero.

🇮🇳 A Nation Mourns

The news of Nana Patekar's passing has sent shockwaves across the nation. Tributes have poured in from every corner — from political leaders to fellow actors, from filmmakers to the common people who saw him as their own.

🎬 Film Industry

Colleagues across Bollywood and Marathi cinema have expressed their grief, remembering his generosity, intensity, and the mentorship he provided to younger actors. Many have called him "irreplaceable" and "the last of the real actors."

🌾 Farming Community

Thousands of farmers who were directly helped by Nana Patekar's Naam Foundation have gathered to pay their respects. For them, he wasn't a celebrity — he was family. His work for the farming community will continue to inspire generations.

🙏 Public Tributes

Social media has been flooded with emotional tributes and clips of his most famous scenes. The hashtag #NanaPatekar has been trending with millions sharing their favourite memories and dialogues.

🕯️

Your Art Will Live On Forever 🎭❤️

His powerful performances, unforgettable dialogues, and unique acting style touched millions of hearts. His contribution to Marathi and Hindi cinema will always be remembered.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

When did Nana Patekar pass away?

Nana Patekar passed away in October 2026 at the age of 75, leaving behind a remarkable legacy spanning decades in Indian cinema.

What are Nana Patekar's most famous movies?

Some of his most iconic films include Krantiveer (1994), Ab Tak Chhappan (2004), Natsamrat (2016), Welcome (2007), Tirangaa (1992), and The Attacks of 26/11 (2013).

What awards did Nana Patekar win?

Nana Patekar won 3 National Film Awards, multiple Filmfare Awards, and several Maharashtra State Film Awards. He was also honoured with the Padma Shri in 2013.

What was Nana Patekar known for apart from acting?

Besides acting, Nana Patekar was deeply known for his philanthropy through the Naam Foundation, which helped drought-affected farmers in Maharashtra. He was also known for his simplicity and down-to-earth personality.

What was Nana Patekar's real name?

Nana Patekar's full name was Vishwanath Patekar. He was born on January 1, 1951, in Murud-Janjira, Maharashtra, India.
